## Confirmed product behavior

Latest attempt per distinct passage; latest five distinct passages only; count descending then most recently seen; no recency weights; maximum two stacked observations. Evidence uses similar misses/across passages. Possible observations stay tentative. Only 5/5 clears the specific category's currently contributing misses. Unrelated categories, old excluded evidence and passage scores remain. Two unique free completions; retries and drills exempt. Seeded next-uncompleted order. Configured US$5/month. Existing-Postgres waitlist. Raw scores only. Public pre-answer responses contain display lengths but no answer keys or diagnostic blank metadata.
